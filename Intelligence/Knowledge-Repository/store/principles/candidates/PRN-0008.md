```yaml
id: PRN-0008
statement: "Strong agitation on the hot charge, then a gentle straight cool pour, front-loads even extraction and then 'caps' it."
domain: agitation
mechanism: >
  Vigorous agitation while the water is hottest maximises early, even extraction (energy when the
  coffee is most soluble). Immediately following with a gentle straight pour of cooler water both
  drops the temperature and avoids further turbulence — halting aggressive extraction before it
  reaches the bitter/drying tail and locking in the brighter, sweeter fraction. Sequence, not just
  amount, of agitation is the lever.
applies_when: ["dual-temperature or staged immersion brews", "want brightness + evenness without over-extraction"]
levers: [agitation, temp]
strategy_implication: "Pair agitation with the hot phase for even energy, then cap with a gentle cool pour to preserve clarity — don't agitate the whole brew uniformly."
```
