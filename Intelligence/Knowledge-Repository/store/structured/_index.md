# store/structured — normalized entries

Raw captures mapped onto schema, machine-comparable.

- `sources/` — `SRC-####.md` (one per source; template: `templates/source-entry.md`)
- `recipes/` — `RCP-####.md` (one per recipe; template: `templates/recipe-entry.md`)

Recipes here are **evidence**, not deliverables — they exist to be mined for principles, never served back to users.

IDs increment globally and never reuse. Empty until ingestion begins.
